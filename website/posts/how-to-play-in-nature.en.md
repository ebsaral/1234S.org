> UNO! is a table (card) game. It is played with at least two players. The aim of the game is to finish the cards in your hand before everyone else. In this card game, there are a total of 112 cards in four different colors (yellow, blue, green, red). Each color includes numbers from 0 to 9. In addition, there are five types of cards that do not carry numbers. When a player has no cards left in hand, the round ends and that person becomes the winner. If you would like more information about the game: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uno_(card_game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uno_(card_game))
